# Radha: Voice-First AI Counter for India's 12 Million Kirana Stores

### The Everyday Problem
Between 6 PM and 9 PM, every local kirana store in India turns chaotic. Customers queue up at the counter, WhatsApp pings non-stop with unorganized voice notes, and incoming phone calls interrupt every billing calculation. The shopkeeper juggles a calculator in one hand, packs groceries with the other, and scribbles delivery addresses on loose scrap paper. 

By closing time, paper slips get lost, item rates get miscalculated, and delivery boys take the wrong bag to the wrong house. Big quick-commerce apps charge steep commissions and demand complicated software setups that neighborhood retailers simply do not want or need.

---

### What Radha Does
Radha turns any retail counter into an automated voice ordering desk with **zero hardware cost**.

1. **Scan to Call**: A customer points their phone camera at a laminated counter QR standee. It instantly opens a web call on their screen without installing any app.
2. **Speaks Like a Shopkeeper**: Radha greets the customer in natural Hindi or Hinglish, listens clearly even with loud grinder and traffic noise in the background, notes down items, and calculates the bill in clean, whole rupees.
3. **Smart Follow-ups**: Instead of making assumptions, Radha asks whether the customer wants home delivery or store pickup, collects their name and room number, and checks if they plan to pay in cash or by UPI.
4. **Auto-Disconnect & Live Alerts**: As soon as the order is confirmed, the call automatically hangs up. The complete itemized bill and address appear immediately on the shop owner's Google Sheet and ping their phone with an instant Telegram receipt card.

---

### Why This Works in the Real World

* **Zero New Devices**: No POS terminals, tablets, or microphone gear. Customers use their own phones, and shopkeepers track everything on their existing Google account.
* **Owner Stays in Control**: The shopkeeper manages prices in a basic Google Sheet. If wheat flour changes from ₹60 to ₹65, Radha quotes the updated price on the very next call.
* **Handles Shop Noise**: Built-in audio filters remove heavy compressor hums, ceiling fan rumble, and street noise so the customer's voice is never misheard.
* **True Indian Retail Math**: Prices are strictly rounded to whole rupees (₹56, never ₹56.0).

---

### The Vision
India's kirana shops already have community trust and 10-minute delivery speed. Radha gives them hands-free digital ordering with nothing more than a sheet of paper on the counter.
